Sample from issue week 42

Three items from the briefing of 15 October 2026.

Counter-drone

Kongsberg will invest over PLN 200 million in a counter-drone plant in Poland; serial production from 2028, tied to the San programme (about PLN 15 billion).

Why it matters: Europe's largest C-UAS programme gets dedicated local production, with exports planned. Source: Defense News

Ukraine

Former minister Fedorov launches an interceptor UAV company against Geran-5, with Palantir as first investor; target is hundreds per month by end-2026.

Why it matters: new funding and scale-up in the drone war. Source: Janes

Funding

European Commission awards EUR 168.2 million to eight EDIP projects for energetics, including drone propulsion and loitering munition warheads.

Why it matters: direct EU funding for the drone supply chain. Source: European Commission

About Defense & Drone Radar

Defense & Drone Radar provides OSINT-based competitive and market intelligence for defense-tech companies; its flagship product is the weekly briefing. Founded by Elias Lorentz, a drone pilot based in the Jura, Switzerland, it combines hands-on drone experience with systematic open-source research: concise, sourced, no hype.

Where does the content come from?

Public sources: trade media, government bodies, EU tenders, company announcements and public posts. Every item is researched, summarised in our own words and linked.
